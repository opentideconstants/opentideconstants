Nine more reasons to switch

  • 0.26min

    Predictions that match the tables

    Given NOAA's own constants, OTC's predictor lands a median 0.26 min from NOAA's own tables. Another open engine lands 2.85 min away. Every OTC SDK must match this predictor to 1 s.

  • 32min

    Wrong clocks caught

    Read the Eider barrier's gauge clock as German time, as the other open database does, and high and low water there come 32 min late on average. OTC checked: the record is UTC. Problem records are labelled and never your default.

  • ±

    Know which number to trust

    Every set says where it came from. Every OTC fit gives an uncertainty for each constituent.

  • 4agencies

    Official constants, checked on every build

    NOAA, Kartverket, Japan's JMA and New Zealand's LINZ. Each is compared with the agency's own predictions on every build, and a source that drifts stops the build.

  • 4SDKs

    One answer in every language

    Python, Ruby, TypeScript and C, held to one conformance suite, plus drop-ins for the tools you already use.

  • 962

    Short records, honest numbers

    962 places with under a year of data are published, each with a measured expected accuracy.

  • 1.0

    Stable and citable

    Format 1.0 with a published schema, station ids that never move, dated releases with SHA-256 checksums, and one DOI.

  • 2,218bins

    Currents at every depth

    853 current stations with 2,218 depth bins, each bin with its own constants.

    Every set at a place, and you choose
