Recommended: the CMEMS record, because a record of instantaneous readings ranks before hourly means. The two WSV records were hourly means labelled as instants; OTC measured the half-hour shift and corrected it.

With commercial_only, the CMEMS set's non-commercial licence leaves it out, and the recommendation moves to the WSV Binnenhafen fit, whose licence allows commercial use. The credit text follows.

In code, commercial_only is one option when you open a release. Every set carries qc_status and qc_flags with their reasons, so a filter is one line.

Run on the OTC_20261011 release-candidate build. A release gives its own numbers, the same in every SDK.

  • Local time. Results are in UTC, or in the station's own zone when you ask. Every station has an IANA time zone.
  • Offline. The SDK checks a release's SHA-256, caches it and then answers with no network. Or bundle a dated release with your app.
  • Fallbacks hidden by default. Sets OTC would not recommend are there, each labelled with its reason, and one option shows them.
