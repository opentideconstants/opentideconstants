NOAA CO-OPS tidal-current constants

What it is
Harmonic constants for tidal currents (velocity, not water level) at NOAA current-meter stations, published through NOAA's metadata API.
Publisher
NOAA CO-OPS (US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration).
What OTC takes
Official current constants: per depth bin, the major and minor axis amplitude and phase, the azimuth of the current ellipse, and the mean current along each axis. NOAA's depth type B means below chart datum, S below surface and U unknown. These need their own record type (quantity “current”), separate from water-level constants. Subordinate current stations publish offsets only, with no constants.
Licence
US Government work in the public domain (17 U.S.C. §105). NOAA's disclaimer says the information “may be used freely by the public”.
Attribution line OTC uses
“Contains NOAA CO-OPS data (public domain). Not an official NOAA product.”
Access and watching
Keyless JSON API: a station list, then one constants request per station. The constants are static and change only when NOAA re-analyses a station, so a weekly watcher hashes each station's response, as for the NOAA water-level constants.
Coverage
US coasts and estuaries. NOAA's current-prediction list had 4,439 stations when checked on 2026-10-07, of which 2,221 are harmonic stations and the rest subordinate.
Rank
Official constants (currents). They are ranked only against other current constants.
