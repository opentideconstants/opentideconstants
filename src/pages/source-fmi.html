FMI mareographs (Finland)

What it is
Water levels from Finnish mareographs on the Gulf of Bothnia and the Gulf of Finland, published through FMI's open-data service.
Publisher
FMI (Finnish Meteorological Institute).
What OTC takes
Gauge records that OTC fits. No harmonic constants are published. The tidal signal here is a few millimetres, so these sets have low confidence scores and are mainly useful for datum and seiche context.
Licence
CC BY 4.0, as stated in the FMI open-data licence.
Attribution line OTC uses
“Contains data from the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI), CC BY 4.0.”
Access and watching
Keyless WFS 2.0 queries, paged by week. FMI sets request quotas per IP address. A weekly fetch of new records.
Coverage
About 14 Finnish mareographs.
Rank
Gauge fit.
