SHOM REFMAR (France)

What it is
Sea-level observations from French tide gauges in the REFMAR and RONIM networks, published by SHOM through its REFMAR data service. Hourly records at Brest go back to 1990.
Publisher
SHOM (France).
What OTC takes
Gauge records that OTC fits. Observations only. SHOM's own harmonic constants and tide predictions are sold under a paid licence, so OTC does not fetch them, compare with them or publish them. The OTC adapter accepts only the REFMAR observation addresses and refuses any other SHOM address. Because there is no open official prediction to compare with, OTC scores its French fits against a held-out year of REFMAR observations.
Licence
Etalab Licence Ouverte 2.0, which is compatible with CC BY, as stated in SHOM's catalogue record for REFMAR. The source must be named as REFMAR. http://dx.doi.org/10.17183/REFMAR#RONIM.
Attribution line OTC uses
“Contains data from REFMAR. http://dx.doi.org/10.17183/REFMAR#RONIM, Licence Ouverte 2.0. Changed: OTC fitted harmonic constants from the sea-level records.”
Access and watching
Keyless documented REST service. One request returns at most 31 days; longer periods use the asynchronous download. The watcher hashes the gauge list and the licence record, and fetches each new full year of records per gauge.
Coverage
176 French gauges. GESLA already carries 106 REFMAR series, so this source mostly adds fresher data at gauges GESLA has.
Rank
Gauge fit.
