Tide constants, fitted and checked.

OpenTideConstants (OTC) fits its own constants from the observed water levels wherever it can publish them, after checking each gauge's clock. It publishes only what a record can resolve, states the uncertainty of every line it fits, and refuses what fails. You get tide data you can check, with a licence you can act on.

  • Clocks checked first. A record whose clock is shown to be wrong, and that cannot be corrected, is not published.
  • Every line earned. Rayleigh and noise tests decide what is fitted, and each fitted constituent carries its uncertainty.
  • A licence on every set, and one option to keep only what you may sell.

Helgoland. The German federal gauge records here are hourly means labelled as instants. OTC found the half-hour error against a second record, corrected it, and ships the evidence with the set.
