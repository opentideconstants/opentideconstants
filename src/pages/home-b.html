Tide data you can build on, and sell.

OpenTideConstants (OTC) gives you one stable format, SDKs in four languages, and a licence on every constant set, so you know what you may ship in a paid product. The constants under it are fitted from the observed water levels wherever OTC can publish its own fit, and checked before they ship.

  • Python, Ruby, TypeScript and C, with one interface and one conformance suite.
  • commercial_only keeps only the sets whose licence allows commercial use.
  • Format 1.0 with a published JSON Schema and a reader gate. Pin a release and it holds still.
