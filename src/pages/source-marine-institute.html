Marine Institute (Ireland)

What it is
The Irish National Tide Gauge Network, published through the Marine Institute's ERDDAP server, with records from 2000.
Publisher
Marine Institute, Ireland.
What OTC takes
Gauge records that OTC fits, using the published quality flags. No harmonic constants are published. The Marine Institute's tide predictions can serve as a cross-check.
Licence
CC BY 4.0, as stated in the dataset metadata.
Attribution line OTC uses
“Contains data from the Marine Institute (Ireland), CC BY 4.0.”
Access and watching
Keyless ERDDAP queries (CSV), near real time. A weekly fetch of new records.
Coverage
About 20 or more Irish coastal ports.
Rank
Gauge fit.
