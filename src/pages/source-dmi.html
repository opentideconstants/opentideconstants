DMI ocean observations (Denmark)

What it is
Sea-level observations at 10-minute intervals from Danish stations, with a historical archive, published through DMI's open-data API.
Publisher
DMI (Danish Meteorological Institute).
What OTC takes
Gauge records that OTC fits. No harmonic constants are published. DMI's own tide predictions can serve as a cross-check. Inner Danish waters have very small tides, so the fits are most useful on the North Sea and Wadden Sea coast.
Licence
CC BY 4.0, as stated in DMI's terms for its free data.
Attribution line OTC uses
“Contains data from DMI (Danish Meteorological Institute), CC BY 4.0.”
Access and watching
Keyless OGC API Features (version 2). DMI limits requests per IP address. A weekly fetch of new records.
Coverage
The station list returned 201 stations: the Danish coast, and some in Greenland and the Faroe Islands.
Rank
