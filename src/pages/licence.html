Licence and attribution

Licence

  • The constants OTC derives are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
  • Derived constants only. OTC publishes harmonic constants fitted from sea-level records. It never redistributes the raw sea-level series. To get the raw data, download it from GESLA; OTC publishes a manifest of checksums so you can check your copy against the one the build used.
  • This follows the practice of TICON-4 (doi:10.17882/109129), which publishes constants derived from GESLA under CC BY 4.0.
  • NOAA constants and offsets are US Government data in the public domain (LicenseRef-PublicDomain-USGov). OTC publishes them unmodified and marks them as NOAA data. They are not presented as an official NOAA product.
  • Kartverket constants are published by Kartverket under CC BY 4.0. OTC converts their phases from UTC+1 to Greenwich and records the conversion.
  • LINZ constants are published by Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand under CC BY 4.0. OTC converts their phases to Greenwich and records the conversion.
  • Every constant set carries its own licence_id and attribution text in the release files.

Attribution

When you use OTC data, credit OpenTideConstants (see Citation) and the upstream providers of the stations you use:

GESLA
Constants fitted from GESLA records: acknowledge the GESLA website and cite Haigh et al. (2023) and Woodworth et al. (2017), as the GESLA downloads page asks. For records that come from UHSLC, also cite Caldwell et al. (2015).
Data providers
Each GESLA record comes from a national or regional agency, and each fitted set names its provider. The release files carry the full attribution text per set.
NOAA CO-OPS
Credit NOAA's Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services for NOAA constants and offsets.
Kartverket
Credit Kartverket (the Norwegian Mapping Authority) for Kartverket constants.
LINZ
Credit Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand, in text, for LINZ constants.

The complete list of providers, with the citation text each one requires, is generated from the data by the release build. A release fails its build if a provider in the data is missing from this list.

Thanks

OTC depends on the work of the agencies that run tide gauges, the GESLA team that assembles their records, and the TICON team, whose constants and methods are the reference this project compares against.
