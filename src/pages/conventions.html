Conventions

A harmonic constant is only meaningful together with its convention. Every OTC constant set points to a declared convention record, and a converter never guesses one.

Phase reference

  • OTC publishes Greenwich phase lags g in degrees, with time in UTC. This is NOAA's phase_GMT convention.
  • A source that gives phases in a local time zone is converted to Greenwich by its adapter, and the conversion is recorded. Example: Kartverket's API gives phases with utcoffset="+01:00".
  • The convention record states the reference as greenwich_utc, or local with the offset, for the source data before conversion.

Astronomical arguments and nodal corrections

  • The reference predictor uses the XTide/NOAA (Schureman) conventions. The equilibrium arguments V0+u and the node factors f are applied at prediction time, per year, from the predictor's tables.
  • Constants fitted by OTC are therefore free of nodal modulation: the fit uses the same f and u that the predictor applies again. Applying them twice, or not at all, is the error behind the OO1 behaviour described on the Why page.
  • A release whose conventions use f_u_at_prediction carries these astronomical tables as astro_tables: for each constituent the release uses, one value per year of V0+u (V0 at 1 January 00:00 UTC plus u at the middle of the year) and of f (at the middle of the year). Each value holds for the whole year and steps at 00:00 UTC on 1 January. The values are computed with congen (David Flater, XTide) after Schureman, Manual of Harmonic Analysis and Prediction of Tides, US C&GS Special Publication 98. The release build checks them against the tables of the XTide harmonics file (TCD) that the reference predictor reads, and stops if any value differs. They are published under CC BY 4.0. A predictor reads them from the release and needs no astronomy code.
  • The convention record names the V0 model (for example schureman_tcd) and the nodal handling (f_u_at_prediction with formula ids, or none). A convention with f_u_at_prediction names its table in astro_table_id.
  • Fitted constants are tied to the predictor's tables by construction. The build records the tables' SHA-256, and a change to the tables triggers a full rebuild.

Constituents that need care

SA (solar annual)
NOAA and the reference predictor use V = h. The Doodson Sa (056 554) is h − p1, which differs by about 77°. TICON-3 SA phases showed this offset (+76° measured); TICON-4 SA phases follow V = h.
M1
Several incompatible definitions exist (Doodson 155 555, 155 655 and the Schureman composite). OTC uses the Schureman composite, as NOAA does. TICON-4 M1 is about −105° from NOAA's M1, and is not used.
OO1
Must be fitted with the same nodal corrections that are applied at prediction time. TICON-4 OO1 on short records behaves as if no effective nodal correction was applied.
EPS2, MKS2
NOAA does not publish them, so they cannot be cross-checked against NOAA.

Units

Amplitudes in metres (tidal-current amplitudes in metres per second), phases in degrees, speeds in degrees per hour, times in UTC. Field names are stable within a major format_version; see Data format.

A current bin's velocity along each axis is a constant plus a harmonic sum. The constant is the bin's mean current, mean_major_ms along the major axis (positive toward azimuth_deg) and mean_minor_ms along the minor axis, in metres per second. It is not a constituent, and no current constituent is called Z0. NOAA's predictions include it: leaving it out moved slack times by up to 34 minutes at the stations checked (ACT1616, BOS1101 and PUG1501).

The convention table

The full constituent table (Doodson numbers, speeds, node-factor formula ids and the IHO standard name where one exists) is in every release, in the constituents field of the JSON file and in OTC_{YYYYMMDD}.meta.json.
